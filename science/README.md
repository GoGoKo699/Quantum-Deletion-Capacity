# Scientific account

The question is whether preshared entanglement can remove the rate penalty caused by hidden deletion positions. The answer is quantified by a finite-block alignment bound and a matching leading small-deletion capacity law.

Read the account in this order:

1. [Operational model](model.md) fixes the one-string channel, charged resources, and reference-stable transmission error.
2. [Alignment geometry and the Choi spectrum](alignment-spectrum.md) derives the exact Gram blocks and the uniform spectral bound.
3. [Coding theorems](coding-theorems.md) connects that bound to arbitrary encoders and gives a direct one-block construction, exact error constants, and the independent-deletion limit.
4. [Interpretation](interpretation.md) explains the positional-uncertainty penalty, a small example, the erasure comparison, and the resource boundaries.

The [related-work account](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) identifies inherited ingredients and the closest models. [Finite verification](../verification/README.md) is available separately; it does not replace the general proofs.
